# User Research Guide

## Seller / service-provider questions

1. What proof do you currently request before accepting warranty/service coverage?
2. What happens if the product is sold to another person?
3. Can coverage be transferred today? If yes, what steps are required?
4. Do multiple repair locations share one entitlement database?
5. How often do receipts or owner records cause delays?
6. Would you accept a portable entitlement issued by another trusted seller?
7. What would make you reject such a system?

## Buyer questions

1. Have you bought or sold a used device with remaining warranty/service coverage?
2. Was the coverage easy to prove or transfer?
3. What proof would you feel comfortable sharing with a repair shop?
4. Would automatic transfer with the product be useful?
5. What would make this workflow confusing or untrustworthy?

## Evidence rule

Do not turn polite interest into fake demand. Record willingness to pilot, actual integration, and actual usage separately.
