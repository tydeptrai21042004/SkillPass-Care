# Phase-1 Non-Goals and Non-Overlap Boundary

SkillPass Care deliberately stays narrower than the surrounding CKB ecosystem.

It is **not**:

- a Fiber payment or spending-permission protocol;
- an x402 protocol implementation;
- a DID, reputation, or credential system;
- a generic token-gating framework;
- a generic agent-authorization framework;
- a marketplace, insurer network, or repair-provider settlement network;
- a proof that the service-right holder physically possesses or legally owns an associated product.

SkillPass Care tests one application question:

> Can mutable service coverage and service history remain continuous when the canonical SkillPass service right transfers from one owner to another and independent providers authorize against the new live owner?

The ownership answer comes from SkillPass. Care owns only application policy/state such as plan, quota, service events and issuer controls.
